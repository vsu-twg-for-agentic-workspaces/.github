# 📜 Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

As members, facilitators, and participants of the **VSU Technical Working Group for Agentic Workspaces**, we are dedicated to providing a respectful, collaborative, and inclusive environment for everyone across the Visayas State University community.

Our conduct is guided by the university's institutional core values:
- **Relevance:** We produce practical, impact-driven tools that serve the university community.
- **Integrity:** We uphold ethical AI practices, honesty, and data protection in all our undertakings.
- **Truth:** We value academic inquiry, factual accuracy, and critical evaluation of AI-generated content.
- **Excellence:** We strive for the highest quality in educational materials, technical workflows, and documentation.

## Standards of Behavior

- **Be welcoming and patient:** Many participants are non-technical educators and staff using AI and version control for the first time.
- **Provide constructive feedback:** Frame code reviews and critiques constructively.
- **Respect privacy:** Honor personal privacy and university confidentiality guidelines.

## Enforcement

Violations of this Code of Conduct may be reported to the facilitator team at `jomarijoseph.barrera@vsu.edu.ph`.
